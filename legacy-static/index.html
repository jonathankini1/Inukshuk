What is an Inukshuk?

An Inukshuk is a stone figure built without mortar. It marks a route or a place. The stones are lifted by a group, not by one person.

We commission this kind of landmark for private land in the UK. Local stone where the ground allows it. A site visit before any build.

How It Works

From the first conversation to the build day.

01

Site Visit

We walk your land together. We look at the ground, the stone, and where it will stand.

02

Stone Selection

Local stone where possible. We source boulders and slabs that match your landscape, including limestone, sandstone, granite, or whatever your land offers.

03

The build

We build it. Our team does the machinery, the lifting and the foundation. You can be there. You do not organise the stone and you do not lift it.

04

After the build

The foundation is in before any stone goes up. We finish the work on site. If more work is needed, we agree it before we leave.

Frequently Asked Questions

The practical details behind creating a permanent landmark on your land.

Where does the stone come from?

We look for stone that belongs to the setting. Where suitable material is on your land, we assess it first. If not, we source from a local quarry or a reclaimed supply.

How long does a project take?

The on-site build is typically one to several days, depending on scale. The full programme also includes the site visit, design, engineering review, stone selection, logistics and ground preparation.

Will it need planning permission?

Requirements vary by height, location, land designation and local authority. We flag likely considerations during the site assessment, but formal planning advice should come from your local planning authority or appointed adviser.

How is the structure made safe?

Every build is designed around its scale, ground conditions and exposure. Larger commissions include an engineering review and a purpose-designed foundation before any stone is lifted into place.

Can family and friends take part?

Yes. Our team manages the machinery, the lifting and the safety. People can help where it is safe.

Where do you work?

We work across the UK and will consider projects further afield. Travel, specialist transport and local logistics are scoped clearly before a commission begins.
